# Showtanz Contest Info

Welcome to the official information hub for the annual **TUKUJU Showtanz Contest**!

## Event Details

* **Date:** November 14, 2026
* **Location:** Tudorfer Bürgerhalle, Salzkotten-Tudorf
* **Admission:** Doors open at 5:00 PM | Start at 6:00 PM

## Categories

Our contest features dance groups competing in two major categories:

1. **Show Dance (Youth):** Ages 12 - 17. High energy, storytelling, and creative themes.
2. **Show Dance (Adults):** Ages 18+. Acrobatic stunts, complex choreography, and stunning outfits.

## How to Register

Registration is open until **October 1, 2026**. Group coordinators can register by submitting the form on our Contact/Registration page or sending an email to our office.
