# Contest FAQ

Here are answers to the most common questions regarding the Showtanz Contest.

### 1. Who can participate?
Any amateur dance group is welcome! We divide categories by age group, so ensure your dancers meet the age requirements for the category you register for.

### 2. What are the stage dimensions?
The stage at the Tudorfer Bürgerhalle is **12 meters wide by 8 meters deep**, with a clear height of **4.5 meters**. Acrobatic throws and high lifts should be planned accordingly.

### 3. Is there a time limit for performances?
Yes. Performances must be between **4 and 8 minutes** long. This includes entrance and exit. Going over or under the time limit may result in point deductions from the jury.

### 4. How is the winner determined?
Our independent panel of judges scores each performance based on four criteria:
- Choreography & Complexity
- Synchronicity & Execution
- Theme & Expression
- Costume & Visual Impact

If you have any other questions, please reach out to us via our contact email!
